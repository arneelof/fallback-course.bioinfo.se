Session 3: live quiz answer key

Proposed 2027 review · 13 November 2026 · Ten conceptual checks

The quiz source uses quizbinf’s four-choice format with mode: once. Correct-answer positions are balanced and randomized, and the distractor order is shuffled reproducibly with seed 20261113. This is a locally prepared quiz, not a change to the running service.

For the final 5–10 minutes, select five questions in advance: 1, 4, 5, 8 and 10 cover shared records, evidence, sequence choice, queries and AI claims. Keep question 1 first. The other five are substitutions or material for a longer session; asking all ten with explanations is likely to take 15–20 minutes. Project aggregate results and allow time to think.

Answers and teaching explanations
QuestionAnswerExplanationGoal
1. Shared archival recordsB: Two access routes to one shared archival recordINSDC partners exchange public records under shared accessions. Seeing the same submitted record through two interfaces does not provide independent experimental evidence.1
2. Identifiers and sequence versionsB: NM_000518.5NM_ identifies a RefSeq mRNA record, and .5 is its sequence version. HBB is a gene symbol, 3043 a gene record identifier, and NP_000509.1 a protein accession.1
3. Sequence versus expressionA: Expression measurements from the relevant sample or conditionA sequence record describes which sequence is represented. Estimating abundance requires expression measurements and their sample or condition metadata.1
4. Claim-level evidenceD: Inspect the evidence attached to the claim and its linked referencesReviewed status describes curation. Individual annotations can have different kinds of support, so assess the evidence for the particular statement.1
5. Choosing a sequence typeC: The genomic span containing the exons and intervening intronsThe genomic span retains introns. A mature spliced transcript joins exons, the coding sequence omits introns and untranslated regions, and a protein sequence contains amino acids.2
6. Tracks and genomic contextC: Display the ClinVar and transcript annotation tracks on the same assembly and intervalTracks align features to a shared coordinate system. Keep the assembly and interval consistent, then follow the feature links to inspect its source and evidence.2
7. Programmatic accessB: A script using a documented programmatic interface, with record checks and saved retrieval detailsProgrammatic access supports repeated retrieval and processing. A useful script checks returned identifiers and fields and records requests and retrieval dates; automation alone does not ensure accuracy.2
8. Building efficient queriesC: (HBB OR "beta globin") AND splicingParentheses group the alternative names before combining them with splicing. Without that grouping, one branch can retrieve HBB records without requiring splicing. Search syntax and phrase handling still need checking in the chosen service.3
9. Citation versus full textD: Full-text availability must be checked separately through the record links, a repository or university accessPubMed principally provides publication records and abstracts. A full-text link may lead to an open repository or a publisher, where university access may be needed.3
10. Checking an AI-attributed claimA: The summary changes an RNA measurement into a percentage of corrected cellsVerify both the publication and the claimed finding. The measurement concerns correctly spliced RNA in a cell model; it does not by itself quantify corrected cells or demonstrate clinical benefit.3
