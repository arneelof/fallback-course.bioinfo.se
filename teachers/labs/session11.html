Section 4: ESM-2 embeddings

Part A learned representations as part of a labelled task: the LSTMs learned their own residue embeddings from signal-peptide labels. Part B uses representations pretrained on millions of unlabelled proteins by a language model, ESM-2. The mean of ESM-2’s last-layer residue vectors gives one embedding per protein (320 numbers for the 8-million-parameter model). Cosine similarity between such mean-pooled embeddings is a useful exploratory measure of similarity, not a calibrated evolutionary distance.
