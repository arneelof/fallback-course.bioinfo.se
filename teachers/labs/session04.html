Step 1 — a global alignment by hand

Use the real fragments seqA = HFGKE from human beta-globin (HBB, UniProt P68871, residues 118–122 in the UniProt sequence) and seqB = HGQE from human myoglobin (MB, P02144, residues 25–28). Put seqA on the rows and seqB on the columns. Use BLOSUM62 and a linear gap score of −4 for each gap position. Initialise S[0,0] = 0, S[i,0] = −4i and S[0,j] = −4j. The full matrix contains six rows and five columns including the empty-prefix borders. Use the notebook’s BLOSUM62 table and check your own rows as you work. After traceback, calculate percent identity and percent similarity by hand. Divide each count by the total number of alignment columns, including gaps, and multiply by 100. For identity, count columns with two identical residues. For similarity in this lab, count columns with two residues whose BLOSUM62 substitution score is strictly positive. Gap columns contribute only to the denominator.
