Part B: reading AlphaFold DB models

The AlphaFold DB model’s B-factor column holds pLDDT (0-100): above 90 very high confidence, 70-90 confident, 50-70 low, below 50 very low (often disorder or flexibility, but also too little information or simply model uncertainty). Answers below refer to the current models (model version 6 in September 2026). If the database has been updated since, use your own notebook’s output.

Question 6 · choose one · 1 point

Question 7 · choose one · 1 point

Region II: a high ipTM for Izumo1 : Juno says that the interface AlphaFold generated is self-consistent and plausible. Does it also show that AlphaFold considered, and ruled out, the other sperm proteins that could bind the same face of Izumo1?

  • Yes: AlphaFold compares all possible partners internally

  • No: AlphaFold only models the chains you give it, so identifying the right partner needs additional information (other predictions, experiments)

  • Yes, if ipSAE is also high

  • No, because ipTM is meaningless for heterodimers

Question 8 · choose one · 1 point

Region IV: p53’s transactivation domain is disordered on its own and folds into a helix when bound to MDM2 (1YCR). If AlphaFold3 predicts this complex with high confidence, which is the most careful interpretation?

  • The model has learned the general physics of disorder-to-order coupled folding

  • It may also have memorised the complex: 1YCR has been in every training set for decades. A held-out test with similar complexes solved after the training cutoff is needed to tell the two apart

  • High confidence proves the peptide is disordered

  • AlphaFold3 cannot model peptides

Question 9 · choose one · 1 point

Region I (Ephrin-A5 : EphB2) and Region III (antibody : antigen) may give similar ipTM values. Why should you not trust them equally?

  • How well confidence tracks true accuracy differs between problem classes: it is reliable where co-evolution constrains the interface (Region I) and much less so for antibody-antigen interfaces, which have no co-evolutionary signal (Region III)

  • ipTM values from different runs cannot be compared at all

  • Antibodies are always predicted correctly

  • Region I targets are always easier to run on the server
