BLAST's five programs

BLAST comes in several programs, each defined by what type the query and database are (nucleotide, or protein) – blastn compares nucleotide sequences and blastp protein sequences. The other three translate in all 6 reading frames on the fly: blastx translates a nucleotide query and searches a protein database, tblastn searches a nucleotide database, translated, with a protein query, and tblastx translates both the nucleotide query and the nucleotide database.
