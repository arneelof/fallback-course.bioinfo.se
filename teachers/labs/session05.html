Profile HMMs: transitions, emissions and paths

Questions 7–9 use the profile-HMM model in the notebook. Transition probabilities come from outgoing transition counts. Match and insert states emit residues; deletion states are silent.

Interpreting biological evidence

The notebook provides saved results, so no online search is needed. A local HBB→myoglobin BLAST example reports E = 63, bit score 10.8 and alignment length 125 with the original two-hit settings. Repeating the same pair without the two-hit requirement finds a 145-residue alignment with E = 5.38e-11 and bit score 45.4. Independently, saved InterPro/Pfam annotations verified on 14 November 2026 assign Globin family PF00042 to HBB residues 27–142 of 147 (E = 1.3e-29) and myoglobin residues 28–141 of 154 (E = 1.2e-20). These are results from different search models and search spaces.
